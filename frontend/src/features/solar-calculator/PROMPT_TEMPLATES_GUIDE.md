# Solar Calculator Prompt Templates Guide

## Overview
The Solar Calculator now includes a comprehensive prompt template system that helps users ask better questions and get more accurate responses from AREC Helios, the AI assistant. These templates are designed specifically for the Philippines solar market and cover various scenarios users might encounter.

## How to Access Prompt Templates

1. Open the Solar Calculator page
2. Click the blue "AI Assistant" floating action button
3. In the chat input field, click the **📚 (Templates)** button on the left side
4. Browse through the categorized prompt templates
5. Select a template or customize it with your specific details

## Template Categories

### 1. Electricity Bill Analysis
**Purpose**: Help users understand their current electricity consumption and calculate solar savings

**Sample Templates**:
- **Monthly Bill Analysis**: "My electricity bill is {amount} pesos per month and I live in {location}, how much would it cost to build a hybrid system with net metering to make my bill zero?"
- **High Bill Investigation**: "My electricity bill is {amount} pesos per month for a {property_type} in {location}. What could be causing this high consumption and how can solar help reduce it?"
- **Bill Breakdown Analysis**: "Help me understand my electricity bill of {amount} pesos. What are the main cost components and how much could I save with a {system_type} solar system?"

**Example Usage**:
```
My electricity bill is 5,000 pesos per month and I live in Batac City, Ilocos Norte, how much would it cost to build a hybrid system with net metering to make my bill zero?
```

### 2. System Sizing & Design
**Purpose**: Help users determine the right solar system size and configuration for their needs

**Sample Templates**:
- **Residential System Sizing**: "I have a {property_type} in {location} with {area} square meters roof space. What size solar system do I need for {usage} kWh monthly consumption?"
- **Commercial System Planning**: "I own a {business_type} in {location} with {area} square meters of roof space. We consume {usage} kWh monthly. What solar system configuration would work best?"
- **Off-Grid System Design**: "I want to go completely off-grid in {location}. My daily consumption is {usage} kWh. What solar system with battery storage do I need?"

**Example Usage**:
```
I have a 2-story house in Cebu City with 80 square meters roof space. What size solar system do I need for 400 kWh monthly consumption?
```

### 3. Financial Analysis
**Purpose**: Help users understand the financial aspects of solar investment

**Sample Templates**:
- **ROI Calculation**: "Calculate the return on investment for a {capacity} kW solar system in {location}. My current electricity rate is {rate} pesos per kWh."
- **Payback Period Analysis**: "How long will it take to break even on a solar investment of {investment} pesos for a {capacity} kW system in {location}?"
- **Financing Options**: "What are the best financing options for a {capacity} kW solar system? I can pay {budget} pesos upfront and need financing for the rest."

**Example Usage**:
```
Calculate the return on investment for a 5 kW solar system in Davao City. My current electricity rate is 12.50 pesos per kWh.
```

### 4. Technical Guidance
**Purpose**: Provide technical advice on solar equipment and installation

**Sample Templates**:
- **Panel Selection**: "What type of solar panels should I choose for {location}? I need {capacity} kW and have {budget} pesos budget."
- **Inverter Comparison**: "Should I choose a string inverter, microinverter, or power optimizer system for my {capacity} kW installation in {location}?"
- **Installation Requirements**: "What are the installation requirements and permits needed for a {capacity} kW solar system in {location}?"

**Example Usage**:
```
What type of solar panels should I choose for Baguio City? I need 4 kW and have 300,000 pesos budget.
```

### 5. Location-Specific
**Purpose**: Address location-specific considerations for solar installations

**Sample Templates**:
- **Weather Impact**: "How does the weather in {location} affect solar panel performance? What should I consider for a {capacity} kW system?"
- **Local Incentives**: "What solar incentives and programs are available in {location}? I'm planning a {capacity} kW residential system."
- **Installation Costs**: "How do installation costs differ in {location} compared to Metro Manila? I need a {capacity} kW system."

**Example Usage**:
```
How does the weather in Tacloban City affect solar panel performance? What should I consider for a 6 kW system?
```

### 6. Maintenance & Troubleshooting
**Purpose**: Help users maintain and troubleshoot their solar systems

**Sample Templates**:
- **Performance Issues**: "My {capacity} kW solar system in {location} is only producing {current_production} kWh monthly instead of the expected {expected_production} kWh. What could be wrong?"
- **Maintenance Schedule**: "What maintenance does my {capacity} kW solar system need in {location}? How often should I clean the panels?"
- **Warranty Questions**: "My solar panels are {age} years old and showing {issue}. What does the warranty cover and what are my options?"

**Example Usage**:
```
My 5 kW solar system in Angeles City is only producing 400 kWh monthly instead of the expected 600 kWh. What could be wrong?
```

## How to Use Templates

### Method 1: Use Example Directly
1. Click on a template category
2. Find the prompt you want to use
3. Click "Use Example" button
4. The example text will be inserted into the chat input
5. Send the message to get AI assistance

### Method 2: Customize Template
1. Click on a template category
2. Find the prompt you want to customize
3. Click "Customize" button
4. Fill in the prompted values for each variable (e.g., amount, location, capacity)
5. The customized prompt will be inserted into the chat input
6. Send the message to get personalized AI assistance

### Method 3: Copy and Modify
1. Click on a template category
2. Find the prompt you want to use
3. Click the copy icon to copy the example to clipboard
4. Paste it into the chat input and modify as needed
5. Send the message to get AI assistance

## Template Variables

Templates use variables in curly braces `{variable_name}` that you can replace with your specific information:

- **{amount}**: Monthly electricity bill amount in pesos
- **{location}**: Your city or province
- **{property_type}**: Type of property (house, apartment, business, etc.)
- **{area}**: Roof area in square meters
- **{usage}**: Monthly electricity consumption in kWh
- **{capacity}**: Solar system capacity in kW
- **{rate}**: Electricity rate in pesos per kWh
- **{investment}**: Total investment amount in pesos
- **{budget}**: Available budget in pesos
- **{system_type}**: Type of solar system (grid-tied, hybrid, off-grid)
- **{business_type}**: Type of business
- **{current_production}**: Current monthly production in kWh
- **{expected_production}**: Expected monthly production in kWh
- **{age}**: Age of solar panels in years
- **{issue}**: Specific issue or problem description

## Best Practices

1. **Be Specific**: Use exact values for amounts, locations, and measurements
2. **Include Context**: Mention your property type, location, and specific needs
3. **Ask Follow-up Questions**: Use the AI's responses to ask more detailed questions
4. **Use Multiple Templates**: Combine different templates to get comprehensive advice
5. **Save Important Responses**: Copy useful information for future reference

## Tips for Better Results

1. **Location Matters**: Always specify your exact location (city and province)
2. **Be Realistic**: Use realistic consumption and budget figures
3. **Ask for Ranges**: Request price ranges rather than exact costs
4. **Consider Weather**: Ask about weather impact on solar performance
5. **Think Long-term**: Ask about maintenance, warranties, and future upgrades

## Example Conversation Flow

1. **Start with Bill Analysis**: "My electricity bill is 6,000 pesos per month in Quezon City..."
2. **Get System Sizing**: "What size system do I need for my 3-bedroom house with 100 sqm roof?"
3. **Understand Costs**: "What would a 5 kW system cost in Quezon City?"
4. **Compare Options**: "Should I choose grid-tied or hybrid for my needs?"
5. **Plan Installation**: "What permits do I need and how long does installation take?"

## Troubleshooting

If templates don't work as expected:
1. Check that all variables are filled in correctly
2. Ensure your location is specific (city and province)
3. Use realistic numbers for consumption and budget
4. Try different templates for the same question
5. Ask follow-up questions to clarify responses
